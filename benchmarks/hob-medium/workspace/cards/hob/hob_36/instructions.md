# Elrond, Moon-Reader

Observe successful activations of abilities of creatures, including mana abilities and Elrond's own activated ability. Casting a creature is not activating an ability. An opponent's activation or an ability whose source is not a creature does not qualify. The trigger is used once each turn when it triggers, even if it is subsequently countered; reentering creates a new permanent with a fresh limit.

Targets for the seven-mana ability are chosen at activation. Choose zero, one or two distinct other nonland permanents you control, including noncreature artifacts and enchantments. Revalidate each target on resolution against the activating controller and its captured zone stint. Elrond cannot target itself.

Remember the exiled objects independently of Elrond and return only those still in the same exile stint at the next end step. They return under their owners' control, not necessarily the activating player's control. A token cannot return after leaving the battlefield. Creating the delayed trigger during an end step means the next end step, not the one already in progress.

Mana abilities resolve immediately. A non-mana trigger they cause during payment waits until the spell's casting procedure is complete before being placed above it on the stack.
